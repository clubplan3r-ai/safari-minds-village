WORLD 01 • SMART MIND

Train the mind.
Make thinking an adventure.

SMART MIND brings soroban, mental arithmetic, memory, logic, chess and intelligence challenges into an active learning environment for ages 6–15.

THE EXPERIENCE

From calculation to cognitive confidence.

The experience is designed around practice, challenge and discovery rather than passive instruction.

Soroban & mental arithmetic

Structured practice using the abacus as a pathway toward faster, more flexible mental calculation.

Memory & concentration

Short challenges that invite children to observe, retain, recall and improve their strategies.

Logic & chess

Puzzles, patterns, chess and reasoning games that make strategic thinking tangible.

Challenge arena

Timed missions and friendly formats can turn individual progress into shared achievement.

WHO IT SERVES

Young learners, families, schools and competition programs.

SMART MIND can support introductory discovery sessions, structured workshops, school visits, camps and future championship formats.

Specific schedules, staffing and competition rules will be confirmed as the Village becomes operational.
