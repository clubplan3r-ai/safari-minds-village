More Than a Camp

SAFARI MINDS VILLAGE is designed as a new generation smart educational tourism destination in Tunisia.

Education meets adventure.

Children and teenagers discover a unique environment where learning becomes an adventure and every challenge becomes an opportunity to grow.

Mental arithmetic, soroban, intelligence games, technology, sports, nature, culture and international competitions come together in one integrated experience.

The objective is not simply to teach children. It is to help them become more confident, creative, curious and capable of leading.

Our Vision

To create an internationally recognized destination where children can learn, explore, compete, innovate and experience Tunisia in a completely new way.


A smart destination for smart generations.
