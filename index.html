THE CONCEPT

We are not rebuilding La Pinède.
We are reimagining the experience.

SAFARI MINDS VILLAGE connects learning and leisure in one coherent experience: children think, play, explore, compete and create while families discover a distinctive Tunisian destination.

01

SMART MIND

Soroban, mental arithmetic, memory, logic, chess and intelligence games.

 02

FUTURE LAB

Robotics, AI for kids, 3D, solar, water technology and STEAM discovery.

 03

SMART FOREST

Nature missions, observation, environmental discovery and teamwork.

 04

BLUE SEA

Coastal discovery, marine awareness and supervised sea experiences.

 05

CHAMPIONS ARENA

Challenges, tournaments, showcases and international competition formats.

 06

TUNISIA EXPERIENCE

History, culture, crafts, creativity and local discovery.
