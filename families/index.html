A destination where curiosity becomes an adventure.

SAFARI MINDS VILLAGE is designed to give children and teenagers a richer kind of leisure experience: think, play, explore, compete and create.

WHY FAMILIES

More than a day out.

The experience connects intelligence games, soroban and mental arithmetic with nature, technology, sport, culture, media and challenge.

Think

Memory, logic, mental arithmetic, soroban, chess and brain challenges.

Explore

Forest missions, coastal discovery and observation-based learning.

Create

Robotics, 3D, media, public speaking and hands-on Future Lab activities.

Challenge

Team missions, tournaments and the spirit of constructive competition.

Discover Tunisia

Culture, history, crafts, creativity and local experiences.

Connect

Family-friendly experiences designed around learning and shared memories.
