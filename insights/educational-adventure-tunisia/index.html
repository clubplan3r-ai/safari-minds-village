SAFARI MINDS VILLAGE – TUNISIA

What is an educational adventure destination?

A destination where children learn through intelligence, nature, technology, sport, culture and challenge.

A different model

SAFARI MINDS VILLAGE – TUNISIA is designed as an educational adventure destination rather than a traditional school or conventional camp. The experience connects thinking, movement, discovery, creativity and challenge.

Eight experience worlds

The concept brings together SMART MIND, FUTURE LAB, SMART FOREST, BLUE SEA, CHAMPIONS ARENA, TUNISIA EXPERIENCE, SMART RESORT and SPEEDOOO MINDS RADIOOO.

Why Tunisia

Tunisia offers a setting where Mediterranean coast, forest, history, culture and modern learning ideas can meet in one destination concept.

For ages 6–15

The core audience is children and teenagers aged 6–15. Activities can be adapted by age, level and group objective, with operational details confirmed for each programme.
