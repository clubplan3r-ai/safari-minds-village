WORLD 03 • SMART FOREST

Explore the forest.
Think like an explorer.

Outdoor missions turn the natural environment into a living learning space for observation, orientation, teamwork and environmental awareness.

Explorer missions

Follow clues, observe details and solve route-based challenges as a team.

Nature detectives

Discover plants, habitats, signs and seasonal changes through guided observation.

Eco challenges

Connect everyday choices with waste reduction, biodiversity and care for shared spaces.

Team navigation

Build communication, planning and decision-making through supervised outdoor missions.

THE PRINCIPLE

The forest becomes part of the lesson.

SMART FOREST is designed to complement, not replace, formal education: children learn by moving, noticing, asking questions and cooperating.

Routes, safety procedures and activity zones will be validated on site before launch.
