LA PINÈDE • BORJ CEDRIA • TUNISIA

A coastal site reimagined for learning and adventure.

The SAFARI MINDS VILLAGE concept is designed around an existing La Pinède environment where the coast, green space and leisure infrastructure can become one connected educational adventure experience.

THE PLACE

From a site to a destination.

The project starts from an existing setting rather than a blank construction site. The ambition is to rehabilitate and connect spaces so that children, teenagers, families, schools and partner organizations can experience learning through movement, nature, technology, culture and challenge.

Operational capacity, services, safety procedures, opening dates and booking conditions will be published only when formally confirmed.

Coast

Blue Sea experiences and marine discovery, delivered with appropriate supervision and partners.

Forest

Outdoor missions, observation, teamwork and environmental learning.

Shared spaces

Workshops, challenges, culture, media and family-oriented experiences.
