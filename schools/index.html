FOR SCHOOLS • GROUPS • EDUCATORS

Learning outside the classroom, by design.

SAFARI MINDS VILLAGE can serve as a future platform for educational visits, workshops, challenges and interdisciplinary experiences for ages 6–15.

EDUCATIONAL PATHWAYS

Connect curriculum themes with real experiences.

The eight worlds create a flexible architecture for visits that combine cognitive skills, STEAM, environment, culture, sport and communication.

Mathematics & cognition

Mental arithmetic, soroban, memory, logic and problem-solving challenges.

STEAM & technology

Robotics, AI for kids, 3D, solar, water technology and smart agriculture concepts.

Environment

Forest observation, ecological missions and coastal awareness.

Culture

History, crafts, creativity and Tunisia-focused discovery.

Sport & teamwork

Movement, cooperation, challenge and supervised activity concepts.

Media & communication

Podcasting, interviews, public speaking, video and youth journalism.

GROUP EXPERIENCE

One visit can connect several worlds.

A school or youth group could combine a cognitive challenge with a Future Lab workshop, an outdoor mission and a cultural activity, depending on the final operating model.

PARTNERSHIP MODEL

Built with educators, not around them.
