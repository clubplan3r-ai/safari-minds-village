WORLD 02 • FUTURE LAB

Build. Test.
Imagine what comes next.

A hands-on STEAM world where young people can explore technology through making, experimentation and real-world challenges.

Robotics & coding

Build-and-test challenges that connect simple programming with physical movement and problem solving.

AI for young minds

Age-appropriate exploration of how intelligent systems work, with emphasis on creativity, questions and responsible use.

3D & digital making

Discover digital design, prototyping and the path from an idea to a physical object.

Solar, water & smart agriculture

Explore practical technologies linked to energy, water efficiency, recycling and resilient food systems.

LEARNING METHOD

Less lecture. More making.

Future Lab activities are envisioned as short missions: understand the problem, design a solution, build a prototype, test it and explain what changed.

Equipment, age groups and workshop capacity will be finalized with technical and educational partners.

Turn curiosity into prototypes.

Future Lab can also become a platform for schools, universities, technology companies and sponsors.
